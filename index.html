Dolly had that magic in her nature — her smile could calm his anger, and her voice could fix the worst days. Small misunderstandings, ego clashes, moments of silence… but no matter how upset they became, their hearts somehow always found each other again. Because some connections are not temporary. Some people become a habit for the soul. Kanak loved her not just for her beauty, but for the comfort she brought into his life. And Dolly, even with all her attitude, secretly loved the way he stayed, cared, and never stopped choosing her. Their story was never perfect like movies. It was real. Messy. Emotional. Cute. Loyal. And maybe that’s what made it special. Because in a world full of temporary feelings, Kanak and Dolly found something that felt like home in each other. ❤️
